## Position

The project uses recognized standards and frameworks as design references. This document records applicability and evidence intent. It does not declare certification, conformity assessment, legal compliance, or regulatory approval.

| ITIL practices | Release, incident, and operational management | runbooks and release governance | Supporting practice only |

## Applicability workflow

1. identify the organizational or contractual requirement;
2. select the exact standard version and scope;
3. map clauses or practices to project controls;
4. identify evidence and gaps;
5. assign control owner;
6. perform authorized review;
7. record exceptions and residual risk;
8. obtain formal assessment where required.

## Prohibited claims

Until formally assessed and authorized, project materials must not claim:

- ISO certification;
- full compliance;
- regulatory approval;
- legal non-repudiation;
- production security approval.
