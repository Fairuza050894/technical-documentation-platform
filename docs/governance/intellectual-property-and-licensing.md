## Current decision

No open-source or proprietary license has been approved in this repository.

Absence of a license does not grant broad rights to use, modify, distribute, sublicense, or commercialize the source. Repository visibility and source availability are not equivalent to licensing permission.

## Required decision before distribution

An authorized owner must determine:

- legal owner of the source and documentation;
- employee, contractor, and third-party contribution rights;
- internal-only or external distribution scope;
- approved license or proprietary notice;
- third-party dependency obligations;
- trademark and naming rights;
- contribution and disclosure policy.

## Candidate outcomes

```text
Proprietary — internal use only
Proprietary — licensed customer distribution
Apache License 2.0
MIT License
Other approved organizational license
```

This list is not a legal recommendation.

## Control

A `LICENSE` file must not be added until the authorized decision is recorded. When approved, update this document, the README, release policy, and distribution process.
